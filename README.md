# Winch & Recovery Sizing

How much pull it takes to winch a stuck 4x4 out, what winch that needs, what snatch blocks do, how your own winch copes as rope piles onto the drum, what it asks of your battery, and the strap, shackle and block ratings to match.

Every number comes from a published recovery method (I4WDTA), a manufacturer (WARN, Crosby), a standard, engineering practice (LEEA) or plain physics, and the Sources page shows each one with the words it comes from. Where nobody publishes a figure, the page says so.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
