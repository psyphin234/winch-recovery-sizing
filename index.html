! Winching can kill

A winch rope, snatch block, shackle, tree protector or anchor that lets go under load can kill or maim. This page is for information only: it can't see your recovery. Your judgement on the scene comes first; a green tick here is not a go-ahead.

Why, and what to do

Gear that lets go under load flies with enough energy to kill. This page works from published figures and a simple model; it can't see the ground, the state of your winch and rope, your recovery points, the anchor or who is standing where.

If anything looks wrong, stop. Use rated gear on rated recovery points, keep everyone well clear and out of line with the rope (source), follow your winch maker's manual, and get proper training.

    Disclaimer

    For information only. Not advice, not a guarantee, not a go-ahead. Recovery loads depend on ground nobody can measure exactly, and winches, ropes, blocks, shackles and anchors vary with make, age, wear and damage. The figures come from a published method, makers' data and physics, and each is linked to its source, but a real recovery can need more.

    Winching is dangerous. The person running the recovery has to judge it on the scene, and that judgement always overrides this page. Use rated recovery points and rated gear, inspect it before every use, keep everyone well clear of the rope, follow your winch maker's manual, and get trained.
