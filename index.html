Winch & Recovery Sizing

How hard your winch has to pull to get you out, and the winch, snatch block, strap and shackles that takes. From a published recovery method, manufacturers' data and plain physics, with a source for every number.

Battery and wiring
Battery
CCA

Ah

On the battery's label. 650 CCA is WARN's minimum for winching.

The battery management system's continuous discharge limit, from the battery's spec sheet. Many 100 Ah batteries are built for far less than a winch draws.

m

One way; the page counts there and back. WARN's ZEON 10 leads are 1.83 m of 2 AWG. A battery in the load bin needs far longer cables.

A

What your alternator gives at the revs you'll winch at. 0 is the worst case: engine off. WARN recommends winching with the engine running.

An estimate, not a guarantee. Recovery loads depend on ground nobody can measure exactly. The figures come from a published method, makers' data and physics, and each is linked to its source, but a real recovery can need more.
